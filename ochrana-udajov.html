2. What data we process and why

Contact form

When you write to us through the form, we process your name, e-mail address, the text of your message, the page language and the address of the page you sent it from. We use the data only to reply to your message. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by ticking the box when you submit the form. We keep the data only for as long as needed to handle your request and the follow-up communication.

Visit counter

When a stop is displayed, an anonymous visit count is increased. We do not store IP addresses or other personal data. So that a visit from one device is not counted more than once a day, the date of the last visit is stored in your browser (localStorage). The browser remembers your chosen language in the same way. These are not tracking cookies, and we use no advertising or analytics tools.

Spam protection

The form is protected by an invisible field for robots, a fill-time check, a limit on the number of messages and, where enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile verification. For the message limit only a one-way code (hash) of the IP address is stored for 1 hour; the IP address itself is not stored.

Technical logs

Each time a page is displayed, the hosting provider (Vercel Inc., USA) processes technical data including the IP address in server logs – this is necessary for the operation and security of the website (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare Turnstile – verification that the form is submitted by a human (where enabled).
